## Why

- Update `src/data/portfolioData.ts` to include the correct, comprehensive list of technologies (JavaScript, TypeScript, Python, FastAPI, React, Next.js, Redux, Node.js, Express, PostgreSQL, Docker, Tailwind, Sass, HTML, CSS).

- Ensure the new design adheres to the Impeccable design system (dark mode, high contrast, crisp borders).
