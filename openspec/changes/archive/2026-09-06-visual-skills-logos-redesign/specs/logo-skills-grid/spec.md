## Purpose

Defines requirements for a logo-driven, highly visual tech stack showcase featuring authentic brand vector icons and polished card aesthetics.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Authentic brand logos for every technology
Each technology in the skills grid SHALL display its authentic brand vector icon/logo with correct brand coloring and proportions.

#### Scenario: Viewing technology cards in the skills section
- **WHEN** the visitor views the skills section
- **THEN** each technology item (JavaScript, TypeScript, Python, FastAPI, React, Next.js, Redux, Node.js, Express, PostgreSQL, Docker, Tailwind, Sass, HTML, CSS) displays a recognizable vector logo and clean label.

### Requirement: Impeccable visual card styling
The skill cards SHALL incorporate subtle brand-themed glows, dark elevation planes, and crisp hover micro-interactions.

#### Scenario: Hovering over tech logo cards
- **WHEN** the visitor hovers over a technology card
- **THEN** the card gently elevates with a subtle border highlight and brand accent glow.
